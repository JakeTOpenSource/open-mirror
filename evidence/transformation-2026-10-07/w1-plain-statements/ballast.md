Open Mirror step 1, plain statement (written in explanation mode; nothing added, nothing dropped). Six cells stand in a line, numbered 1 (upstream) to 6 (downstream). Each cell has a whole-number level L that is never negative, a fixed capacity C, an upper trigger U and a lower trigger D with D < U <= C, and a mode that is either NORMAL or ELEVATED. Every cell starts NORMAL. There is one global limit G and one running tally LOST, which starts at 0. The process runs exactly 5 periods. Each period has five steps, always in this order, and each step finishes for all cells before the next step starts. STEP 1, INTAKE: each cell's level goes up by that period's amount for that cell from the intake table. Capacity is not enforced, so a level may be above capacity afterwards. STEP 2, SPILL-OVER: cells are handled one at a time in order 1 to 6. If a cell's level is above its capacity, the excess (L minus C) is added to cell n+1 and the cell's level becomes exactly C. Excess from cell 6 is added to LOST. Because the order is 1 to 6, excess reaches the next cell before that cell is handled, so it can push that cell over capacity in the same step, and so on down the line. STEP 3, MODE CHECK, using levels after spill-over: a NORMAL cell with L >= U becomes ELEVATED. An ELEVATED cell with L <= D becomes NORMAL. In every other case the mode stays the same, so a cell between D and U keeps its mode. Each change is recorded as (period, cell, old mode, new mode). A cell changes at most once per period. STEP 4, RELEASE: first compute all six release amounts from the levels at the start of this step: floor(L/4) for a NORMAL cell and floor(L/2) for an ELEVATED cell. Then apply all six at once. Each cell's level drops by its own amount. A NORMAL cell's amount goes to LOST. An ELEVATED cell's amount is added to cell n-1, and if cell 1 is ELEVATED its amount goes to LOST. Capacity is not enforced, so a cell may be above capacity after it receives a transfer. That is corrected only at the next period's spill-over, and never if it happens in period 5. STEP 5, GLOBAL RESET: sum the six levels. If the sum is strictly greater than G, every level becomes floor(L/2), every removed amount is added to LOST, and the period is recorded as a reset. If the sum is equal to G or less, nothing happens. After period 5 the output is: the final levels in order 1 to 6; the final modes in order 1 to 6; the full list of mode changes as (period, cell, old, new); the periods in which a reset happened; the final LOST; and the balance check, (sum of starting levels) + (sum of all intake) = (sum of final levels) + LOST.
