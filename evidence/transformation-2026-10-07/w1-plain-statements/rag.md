Mode: explanation mode (Open Mirror step 1 applied to the engineer's process). Six positions in a line, numbered 1 (upstream) to 6 (downstream). Each holds a whole-number amount, never negative, and has a fixed capacity C, an upper trigger U and a lower trigger D (D < U <= C), and one of two states, resting or raised. All start resting. There is one global limit G and a loss tally that starts at 0. The run is exactly 5 cycles of five steps, always in this order; each step finishes for all six positions before the next starts. Step 1: each position's amount rises by that cycle's table entry; capacity is not enforced, so an amount may exceed capacity. Step 2: handle positions in order 1 to 6; if an amount is above capacity, the excess goes to the next position downstream (n+1) and the amount becomes exactly capacity; excess from position 6 goes to the loss tally; because handling is in order, excess arriving from upstream can push the next position over in the same step, cascading. Step 3: using amounts after step 2, a resting position with amount >= U becomes raised; a raised position with amount <= D becomes resting; otherwise (including amounts between D and U) the state is unchanged; record every change as (cycle, position, before, after); at most one change per position per cycle. Step 4: first compute all six amounts from the amounts at the start of this step, before changing anything: resting = floor(amount/4), raised = floor(amount/2). Then apply all at once: each position loses its own amount; a resting position's amount goes to the loss tally; a raised position's amount goes to the position immediately upstream (n-1); a raised position 1's amount goes to the loss tally. Capacity is not enforced; a position can sit above capacity after receiving, corrected only at the next cycle's step 2, and never if it happens in cycle 5. Step 5: sum all six amounts; if the total is strictly greater than G, every amount becomes floor(amount/2), all removed amounts go to the loss tally, and the cycle is recorded as a reset; if the total is equal to or below G, nothing happens. Output after cycle 5: final amounts 1 to 6; final states 1 to 6; complete list of state changes as (cycle, position, before, after); cycles with a reset; final loss tally; balance check: (sum of starting amounts) + (sum of all table entries) = (sum of final amounts) + loss tally.
