None present. 'Uptake' replaces intake, 'push-up' the overflow step, 'draw-down' the outflow step, 'loss tally' the tally.
- Real-plant mislead, SINK-ACTIVE: a real sink draws sap in, but on this line a SINK-ACTIVE segment gives half its sap away down the phloem. The text says it 'gives' half and the sap 'goes down the phloem into the next segment toward the root', one segment only.
- Real-plant mislead, phloem direction: in a real plant, phloem flows toward wherever the sinks are. Here it always goes exactly one segment toward the root. Stated.
- Real-plant mislead, transpiration: in a real plant, transpiration never stops. Here sap leaves only by segment 6 excess, respiration of QUIET draw-down, SINK-ACTIVE draw-down out of segment 1, and leaf-drop. Added 'Nothing else leaves the stem.'
- Real-plant mislead, leaf-drop: a real leaf drop is seasonal and takes leaves. Here it is checked every cycle as step 5, fires only when the six-segment total is strictly above 38, and halves every segment, the root collar included. All stated.
- Real-plant mislead, xylem: a real xylem moves sap up all the time. Here only the excess over holding volume moves, in order 1 to 6, and the excess can cascade. Stated, including that the receiving segment is worked after the sap arrives.
- Overfill: the reader could assume holding volume always applies. The text says uptake can take a segment over, and a segment receiving phloem sap can sit over until next cycle's push-up, or for good after cycle 5.
- Simultaneity: the reader could apply draw-down segment by segment. The text says to work out all six amounts first, from the sap and states as they stand, then apply them all at once.
- Rounding and amounts: QUIET a quarter, SINK-ACTIVE half, rounded down. Leaf-drop halving is rounded down, and the removed sap goes on the tally. Whole units are stated in the opening.
- State check: the marks are inclusive (at or above, at or below), the state holds between the marks, and the check runs on sap after push-up. Each change is logged as (cycle, position, before, after). 'At most once per cycle' is not stated outright; it follows from there being one check per segment per cycle.
- Never negative: not stated outright, to save words. It follows because only fractions, rounded down, are ever removed.
- Report paragraph: confirmed it is a template only, with no figures, totals or results. It lists end sap 1 to 6, end states 1 to 6, the state changes, the leaf-drop cycles, the loss tally and the balance check. Positions run root collar 1 to crown 6, and cycles are numbered 1 to 5 in the table and the steps.
