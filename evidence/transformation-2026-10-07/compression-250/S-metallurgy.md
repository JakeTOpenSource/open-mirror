Crucible 1 is the charging end, 6 the tapping end. Everything is whole units; splits round down.

Working volume, crucibles 1 to 6: 10 8 12 6 9 7. Upper mark: 7 6 9 5 7 5. Lower mark: 3 2 4 2 3 2. Starting melt: 3 6 6 2 2 1. All BASE; slag empty.

Charges, cycles 1 to 5: 0 6 3 5 3 3; 4 2 0 1 1 7; 4 4 3 0 0 6; 7 6 7 1 3 0; 0 6 6 3 3 0.

Each cycle, in order, each step on all six before the next:

Charge; overfull allowed.

Overflow, 1 to 6 in turn: excess over working volume runs down the launder into the next, which may then overflow; 6's to slag.

State check: BASE at or above upper mark goes SUPERHEATED; SUPERHEATED at or below lower mark goes BASE; otherwise unchanged. States change only here. Log changes.

Tap, all draws figured before any move: BASE, a quarter to slag; SUPERHEATED, half up the return launder to the previous crucible, 1's to slag. Overfull waits for the next overflow; after cycle 5, it stays.

Emergency tap if the six total over 38: halve all, removed melt to slag; log the cycle.

Report after cycle 5: end melt and states, 1 to 6; every state change as (cycle, position, before, after); emergency tap cycles; slag tally; balance check: starting melt plus charges equals end melt plus slag.
