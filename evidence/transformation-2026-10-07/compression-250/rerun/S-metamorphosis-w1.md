Chambers 1 to 6, in line, hold whole units of nutrient mass. Lists run 1 to 6.

Limits: 10 8 12 6 9 7. Upper marks: 7 6 9 5 7 5. Lower marks: 3 2 4 2 3 2. Start: 3 6 6 2 2 1, all LARVAL.

Feed, cycle 1 first: 0 6 3 5 3 3; 4 2 0 1 1 7; 4 4 3 0 0 6; 7 6 7 1 3 0; 0 6 6 3 3 0.

Five cycles; each step finishes on all chambers before the next:

Feed: add the row, PUPAL chambers too, ignoring limits.
Carry forward: 1 to 6 in turn, excess over limit moves into the next chamber, possibly overfilling it; chamber 6's is discarded.
State check: LARVAL at or above upper mark goes PUPAL; PUPAL at or below lower mark returns to LARVAL, routinely. Otherwise unchanged; log changes.
Metabolise: figure all shares before moving any, rounded down. LARVAL burns off a quarter; PUPAL passes half back one chamber; chamber 1's is discarded. Overfill waits for next carry forward; after cycle 5, none.
Moult: line total over 38, each chamber keeps half, rounded down, the rest discarded; states unchanged. 38 or under: nothing.

Report. After cycle 5 hand in: end amounts and states, chambers 1 to 6; every state change as (cycle, chamber position, before, after); cycles the moult fired; loss tally (all mass discarded or burned off); balance check: start plus all feed equals end plus loss tally.
