Each cycle, in order, each step on all six before the next:

Charge the cycle's row, ignoring volume.

Overflow, 1 to 6 in turn: excess over volume goes by launder to the next crucible before its turn. Crucible 6's goes to slag.

State, by quantity alone: BASE at or above upper mark goes SUPERHEATED; SUPERHEATED at or below lower mark goes BASE; otherwise unchanged.

Return. First figure all six amounts from current contents, rounded down: BASE a quarter, to slag; SUPERHEATED half, up the return launder one crucible toward charging, crucible 1's to slag. Move all together. Overfill waits for next cycle's overflow; none follows cycle 5.

Emergency tap: if total melt exceeds 38, each keeps half, rounded down; rest to slag.

Report. After cycle 5: end quantities and states, crucibles 1 to 6; every state change as (cycle, position, before, after); cycles the tap fired; slag tally; balance check: starting melt plus charges equals end melt plus slag.
