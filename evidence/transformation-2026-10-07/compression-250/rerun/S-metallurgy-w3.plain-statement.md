Six cells in a line, 1 upstream to 6 downstream. Each cell has a level L (whole number, never negative), a fixed capacity C, an upper trigger U and a lower trigger D with D < U <= C, and a mode, NORMAL or ELEVATED; all start NORMAL. One global limit G; one tally LOST, starting at 0. The process runs exactly 5 periods, each of five steps in this order, each step finished for all cells before the next starts. Step 1 intake: each cell's level rises by that period's table amount for it; capacity not enforced, level may exceed capacity. Step 2 spill-over: cells handled one at a time, 1 to 6; if level > capacity, the excess (L - C) is added to cell n+1 and the level becomes exactly C; cell 6's excess goes to LOST; because of the order, one cell's excess can push the next over capacity in the same step, and so on down the line. Step 3 mode check, on levels after spill-over: NORMAL with L >= U becomes ELEVATED; ELEVATED with L <= D becomes NORMAL; otherwise unchanged (between D and U the cell keeps its mode); record each change as (period, cell, old mode, new mode); at most one change per cell per period. Step 4 release: first compute every cell's amount from its level at the start of the step, NORMAL floor(L/4), ELEVATED floor(L/2), all six before changing anything; then apply all at once: each cell's level drops by its own amount; a NORMAL cell's amount goes to LOST; an ELEVATED cell's amount is added to cell n-1, and cell 1's (if ELEVATED) goes to LOST; capacity not enforced, overfill is corrected only at the next period's spill-over, never if it happens in period 5. Step 5 global reset: sum the six levels; if the total is strictly greater than G, every level becomes floor(L/2), every removed amount goes to LOST, and the period is recorded; if equal to G or less, nothing. Output after period 5: final levels 1 to 6; final modes 1 to 6; full list of mode changes (period, cell, old, new); list of periods with a reset; final LOST; balance check: sum of starting levels + sum of all intakes = sum of final levels + LOST.
