All start LARVAL. Moult threshold: 38.

Per cycle, in order, each step finishing line-wide before the next:
1. Feed: add the row, ignoring limits.
2. Carry-forward, 1 to 6 in turn: excess over limit moves into the next chamber, possibly pushing it over. Chamber 6's excess is discarded.
3. State: LARVAL at or above upper mark turns PUPAL; PUPAL at or below lower mark returns LARVAL, routinely. Otherwise unchanged. Log changes.
4. Metabolism, all at once from pre-step masses: LARVAL burns a quarter; PUPAL passes half back one chamber, chamber 1's discarded. Limits ignored; overfill waits for next carry-forward, or stays after cycle 5.
5. Moult: line total above 38 halves every chamber, removed mass discarded.
Loss tally: everything discarded or burned.

Report: after cycle 5 hand in: end masses, 1 to 6; end states, same order; every state change as (cycle, position, before, after); moult cycles; loss tally; balance check: start plus all feed equals end plus loss tally.
