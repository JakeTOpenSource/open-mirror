Six stem segments: 1 at the root collar, 6 at the crown. Each holds whole units of sap and is QUIET or SINK-ACTIVE; all start QUIET. Everything transpired or respired goes on the loss tally, starting at 0.

Per segment, 1 to 6:
Holding volume: 10, 8, 12, 6, 9, 7
Upper mark: 7, 6, 9, 5, 7, 5
Lower mark: 3, 2, 4, 2, 3, 2
Starting sap: 3, 6, 6, 2, 2, 1
Stem limit: 38

Uptake, segments 1 to 6:
Cycle 1: 0, 6, 3, 5, 3, 3
Cycle 2: 4, 2, 0, 1, 1, 7
Cycle 3: 4, 4, 3, 0, 0, 6
Cycle 4: 7, 6, 7, 1, 3, 0
Cycle 5: 0, 6, 6, 3, 3, 0

Five cycles; each runs these five steps in order, every step finished on all segments before the next.

1. Uptake: add each segment's uptake for the cycle; it may go over its holding volume.

2. Push-up: work segments 1 to 6 in turn. Sap over a segment's holding volume goes up the xylem into the next segment toward the crown, leaving that segment full; segment 6's excess is transpired. What arrives counts before that segment is worked, so excess can carry several segments up.

3. State check, on sap after push-up: QUIET at or above its upper mark turns SINK-ACTIVE; SINK-ACTIVE at or below its lower mark turns QUIET; otherwise, including between the marks, the state stays. Log each change as (cycle, position, before, after).

4. Draw-down: first, from sap and states as they stand, work out all six amounts, rounded down: QUIET a quarter, SINK-ACTIVE half. Then each gives its amount, all at once. QUIET sap is respired. SINK-ACTIVE sap goes down the phloem into the next segment toward the root, which may then sit over holding volume until next cycle's push-up, or for good after cycle 5; from segment 1 it goes on the tally.

5. Leaf-drop: if the six total strictly above 38, halve every segment, rounded down, the removed sap transpired; log the cycle.

Nothing else leaves the stem.

Report. After cycle 5, hand in: end sap per segment, positions 1 to 6; end states, 1 to 6; every state change as (cycle, position, before, after); the cycles leaf-drop fired; the loss tally; and the balance check: starting sap plus all uptake equals end sap plus loss tally.
