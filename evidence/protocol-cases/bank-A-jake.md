# Bank A, set 1: Jake's eight (6 October 2026). Cases only. Key is in keys/bank-A-jake.md (not yet written).

1. Our codebase is like a house. For three years we kept adding rooms without touching the foundation, and now every new room cracks the walls a little more. The foundation is the old auth module everything sits on. We have to stop all feature work and fix the foundation, or the whole house comes down within a year.

2. The national debt is like a maxed-out credit card. We keep borrowing to pay the interest on what we already borrowed. Any household that spent this way would be bankrupt by now. The only responsible move is to cut up the card: slash spending until the balance comes down.

3. Our moderation system is like an immune system having an allergic reaction. It attacks harmless posts, the pollen, while actual harassment walks right through. The answer is immunosuppressants: turn down the sensitivity across the board until the swelling goes down, then rebuild.

4. This company is like a sports team with too many coaches and not enough players. The coaches draw up plays all day while the players are exhausted on the field. Cut half the management layer and put everyone back in the game.

5. Your brain is like a computer with too many tabs open. Every side project is another tab eating memory, and that's why you can't focus. Close the tabs, quit two of the projects, and the machine will run fine again.

6. The market is like evolution. Weak companies die, strong ones survive, and the ecosystem gets fitter every cycle. Bailouts are like keeping a sick animal alive through winter: it survives, but it weakens the whole herd. Let the weak fail.

7. A strong password is like a decent lock on your front door. It won't stop a determined burglar with time and tools, but it stops someone just trying handles. I'm only claiming the casual-thief part: make yourself not the easiest target.

8. Learning Spanish feels like when I learned guitar. The first month feels impossible in both, and then something clicks around week six. I'm not saying the study methods transfer, just that the frustration curve rhymes, so don't quit in week three.
