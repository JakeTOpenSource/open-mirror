Six chambers, positions 1 to 6, all starting LARVAL. Loss tally: all burned or discarded mass, from 0.

Cycles 1 to 5 each run these steps in order, finishing all chambers before the next step:

Feed: every chamber gets its table amount, whatever its state; capacity ignored.

Carry: chambers 1 to 6, in turn, pass excess over capacity to the next, which may overflow; chamber 6's excess is discarded.

Check, after carry: LARVAL at or above upper mark turns PUPAL; PUPAL at or below lower mark returns to LARVAL, routinely. Otherwise unchanged. Log changes (cycle, position, before, after).

Metabolise, shares rounded down and figured before anything moves: LARVAL burns a quarter; PUPAL passes half back to the chamber before it, chamber 1's discarded. Overfill waits for next cycle's carry (none after cycle 5).

Moult: if the six total over 38, every chamber keeps half, rounded down, the rest discarded; log the cycle.
