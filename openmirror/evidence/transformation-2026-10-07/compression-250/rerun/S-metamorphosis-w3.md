Six rearing chambers in line, 1 first, 6 last, hold whole units of nutrient mass. Each is LARVAL or PUPAL, starting LARVAL. Loss tally starts at 0.

By position: capacity 10 8 12 6 9 7; upper mark 7 6 9 5 7 5; lower mark 3 2 4 2 3 2; start 3 6 6 2 2 1.

Five cycles of these steps, in order; each step finishes everywhere before the next.

Feed, by position, capacity ignored, PUPAL included. Cycle 1: 0 6 3 5 3 3; 2: 4 2 0 1 1 7; 3: 4 4 3 0 0 6; 4: 7 6 7 1 3 0; 5: 0 6 6 3 3 0.

Overflow: chambers 1 to 6 in turn pass excess over capacity to the next, which may overflow. Chamber 6 overflows to the tally.

Check: LARVAL at or above its upper mark turns PUPAL; PUPAL at or below its lower mark returns to LARVAL, routinely. Otherwise no change.

Metabolism, all amounts figured before anything moves, rounded down: LARVAL loses a quarter to the tally; PUPAL reabsorbs half its mass back into the previous chamber, chamber 1 into the tally. Overfull chambers wait for next overflow.

Moult: line total over 38 halves every chamber, rounded down, removed mass tallied.

Report. After the last cycle hand in end quantities and states by position; every state change as (cycle, position, before, after); moult cycles; loss tally; balance check: start plus all feed equals end plus tally.
