Six forms on the chute: form 1 under the hopper, form 6 at the end. Mix counts in whole units. Every form starts GREEN.

Forms 1 to 6:
Capacity: 10, 8, 12, 6, 9, 7
Upper mark: 7, 6, 9, 5, 7, 5
Lower mark: 3, 2, 4, 2, 3, 2
Starting mix: 3, 6, 6, 2, 2, 1
Washout limit: 38

Pours, forms 1 to 6:
Cycle 1: 0, 6, 3, 5, 3, 3
Cycle 2: 4, 2, 0, 1, 1, 7
Cycle 3: 4, 4, 3, 0, 0, 6
Cycle 4: 7, 6, 7, 1, 3, 0
Cycle 5: 0, 6, 6, 3, 3, 0

Five cycles, five steps each, in this order. Each step finishes on all six forms before the next.

1. Pour. Each form takes its units, even past capacity.

2. Overtop. Go form 1 to form 6, one at a time. Anything over capacity runs into the next form down; the form is left exactly full. Form 1's overtop is already in form 2 when you reach it, so it can cascade. Form 6 overtops into the waste pit.

3. State check, on what's in the forms now. GREEN at or above the upper mark goes HOT. HOT at or below the lower mark goes back to GREEN; here it does. Anything else keeps its state. One change per form per cycle at most. Log each as (cycle, position, before, after).

4. Bleed and evaporation. Figure all six amounts from current contents first, then move them together. GREEN loses a quarter, rounded down, to evaporation: waste. HOT bleeds half, rounded down, back up the chute into the form before it (5 into 4). Form 1's bleed is waste. A form can bleed and receive in the same step. No capacity limit: overfill waits for next cycle's overtop, and after cycle 5 it stays.

5. Washout. Total all six. Strictly over 38: each form keeps half, rounded down; the rest is waste; note the cycle. Otherwise nothing.

All waste (pit, evaporation, form 1's bleed, washout) goes on the loss tally, starting at zero.

Report. After cycle 5, hand in: the six end quantities, positions 1 to 6; the six end states; every state change as (cycle, position, before, after); the cycles the washout fired; the loss tally; and the balance check: starting mix plus all pours must equal end quantities plus loss tally.
