Six chambers, 1 to 6; overflow runs toward 6, reabsorption back toward 1. Mass is whole units, never below zero. Every chamber starts LARVAL; the loss tally starts at zero.

Chambers 1 to 6:
Holding limit: 10, 8, 12, 6, 9, 7
Upper mark: 7, 6, 9, 5, 7, 5
Lower mark: 3, 2, 4, 2, 3, 2
Starting mass: 3, 6, 6, 2, 2, 1
Line limit: 38

Feeding table, chambers 1 to 6:
Cycle 1: 0, 6, 3, 5, 3, 3
Cycle 2: 4, 2, 0, 1, 1, 7
Cycle 3: 4, 4, 3, 0, 0, 6
Cycle 4: 7, 6, 7, 1, 3, 0
Cycle 5: 0, 6, 6, 3, 3, 0

A run is five cycles of five steps, in order; each step finishes on all six chambers before the next.

1. Feed. Add that cycle's table amount to every chamber, PUPAL included. The holding limit doesn't apply here.

2. Overflow. Chamber by chamber, 1 through 6: anything over the holding limit moves into the next chamber, leaving the chamber exactly at its limit. Chamber 1's excess lands in 2 before 2 is checked, so overflow cascades. Excess from 6 is discarded onto the loss tally.

3. Marks. Weigh after overflow. LARVAL at or above the upper mark goes PUPAL. PUPAL at or below the lower mark goes back to LARVAL; that's routine on this line. Otherwise no change. At most one change per chamber per cycle. Log each as cycle, chamber, before, after.

4. Burn-off. Work out all six amounts from current contents before touching anything. LARVAL metabolises a quarter of its mass, rounded down, onto the loss tally. PUPAL reabsorbs half, rounded down, back into the previous chamber; chamber 1's goes on the loss tally. Apply all six together. No holding limit here either: excess waits for next cycle's overflow, so after cycle 5 it stays.

5. Moult. Total the line. Strictly over 38, every chamber is halved, rounded down, the removed mass onto the loss tally. At 38 or under, nothing.

Report. After cycle 5, hand in: the six end masses, chamber 1 to 6; the six end states, LARVAL or PUPAL; every state change as (cycle, chamber, before, after); the cycles a moult fired; the loss tally; and the balance check: starting mass plus all feed equals end mass plus loss tally.
